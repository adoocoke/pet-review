产生错误的题目

华研《剑桥KET阅读》上册 p.135 · Central Park 完型 第 23 题

错题 / 拿不准
The park is also very 23 with people who like sport.

选择能填进空格的词。

受……欢迎
点击卡片看英文词组
popular with
The park is popular with people who like sport.

同页搭配

  • known by — 被……所熟知
  • visit — 20 million people visit it each year.
  • many + 可数名词 — many tall trees
  • different kinds — 不同种类
  • used for — The road is used for running.
错因

为什么不是 beautiful / big？

空格后面是 with people，固定搭配是 be popular with somebody。

popular with = 受……欢迎，后面加人。

beautiful / big 可以形容公园，不能接 with people。
